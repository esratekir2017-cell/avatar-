# VELORA Avatar AI — Commercial Reels Production Standard

## Mission
Produce professional, commercial-grade brand visuals, product campaigns, Instagram Reels, and 15/30/60-second advertising films. The user wants every suitable free-tier application to support specialized agents. Free website access does not imply free API access or permitted automation.

## Canonical identity — non-negotiable
The approved Esra avatar is IMG_7316.jpeg, OpenArt ID 5AnoW2agSbonaMWxQ8uK. Preserve original face, eyes, facial proportions, short dark bob/bangs, skin details, and identifiable features. Do not substitute a newly generated model, including a long-haired storyboard woman, unless explicitly approved as a different character. Storyboards are creative plans, not a replacement for the identity master. No model can guarantee zero identity drift; require user-approved QC.

## Commercial-grade workflow
1. Creative brief and intended channel (Reels 9:16; widescreen brand film 16:9).
2. Brand/character bible and rights review: original logo, model consent, commercial rights, licenses.
3. Identity-locked keyframes and approved character reference sheet.
4. Shot-by-shot storyboard and production plan.
5. Video scene generation with reference images and repeatable prompts.
6. Continuity review: face, hair, outfit, anatomy, product, lettering, duration, lighting, composition.
7. Editorial assembly, transitions, sound, captions, native aspect ratio and safe zones.
8. Export QC and user approval.
9. Archive source assets in authorized workspace; store manifests, prompts, versions and decisions in GitHub.

## Agent responsibilities
- Orchestrator: priorities, approvals, spending and routing.
- Character/Identity: consistent model, multi-angle references, reject drift.
- Image/Product: product photos, packaging and hero frames.
- Film: cinematic shots, camera and continuity.
- Editor: assemble shots, sound and delivery.
- Brand/Legal: exact logos, commercial rights, music, likeness and model releases.
- QA: technical and visual checks.
- Archivist: versioned metadata and asset pointers.
